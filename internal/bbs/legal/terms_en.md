2. The BBS is strictly opt-in. It is available only in servers where an administrator has enabled it and to users who have agreed to these Terms, the Community Guidelines, and the Privacy Policy.

Article 2 (Local vs. Global Boards)
1. Boards under Guild Local categories are created and moderated by the administrators of that specific Discord server.
2. Boards under Global categories are created and moderated by gobot operators. Threads and replies posted to Global boards are synchronized across all subscribed Discord servers.
3. By posting to a Global board, you acknowledge that your post content will be distributed and displayed across every server subscribed to that board.

Article 5 (Updates and Withdrawal)
1. gobot operators may update these Terms by bumping the policy version, which requires re-acceptance before further posting.
2. You may withdraw your consent at any time using the policy command to stop using interactive BBS actions.
