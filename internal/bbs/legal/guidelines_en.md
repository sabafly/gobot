# gobot Anonymous BBS Community Guidelines

1. Basic Etiquette
- Be respectful of anonymous bulletin board etiquette and keep discussions constructive.
- Enter `sage` in the name field if you do not want a reply to bump the thread.
- Use `>>1` anchor syntax (up to 10 per reply) to reference earlier or upcoming replies.

2. Prohibited Content
- Illegal content, promotion of violence, or self-harm material.
- Doxxing or sharing private personal information (real names, addresses, phone numbers, private accounts) without consent.
- Targeted harassment, defamation, threats, or hate speech.
- Posting links to NSFW, gore, sexually explicit, or harmful media.
- Flooding, spamming, or abusing automated scripts to disrupt threads.

3. Reporting Violations
- Use the "BBS: Report" context menu or the "Report" button at the bottom of a thread to flag rule violations.
- Violating posts may be removed ("Abone") and abusive posters may be banned locally or globally.
