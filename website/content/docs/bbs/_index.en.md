gobot's Anonymous BBS feature brings a 5ch-style anonymous bulletin board directly into Discord forum and text channels.

It supports both server-exclusive **Guild-Local Boards** and **Global Boards** synchronized across multiple Discord servers and federated gobot instances.

## Before You Start

- The Anonymous BBS feature is **opt-in**. Users must consent to the [BBS Terms of Service](/en/bbs/terms/), [BBS Community Guidelines](/en/bbs/guidelines/), and [BBS Privacy Policy](/en/bbs/privacy/) before posting or reporting.
- For complete slash command options, see [Command Reference: `/bbs`](/en/docs/commands/bbs/).
