# gobot Anonymous BBS Community Guidelines

These Guidelines establish the standards of conduct required to keep the gobot Anonymous BBS safe and enjoyable. All users must comply with these Guidelines in addition to the Terms of Service and the Discord Community Guidelines.

## 1. Basic Etiquette

- Respect anonymous bulletin board culture and engage in civil discussion and casual chat. Anonymity is not a shield for attacking others or evading rules.
- To reference another post, use two half-width greater-than signs followed by the post number (e.g., `>>1`).

## 2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following content and actions are strictly prohibited:

1. **Illegal Acts and Dangerous Behavior**
   - Illegal information, threats, incitement, or facilitation of crimes, and transactions involving illegal drugs or weapons.
   - Content that encourages, promotes, or induces self-harm, suicide, or harm to others.
2. **Privacy Violations and Doxxing**
   - Unauthorized disclosure of personally identifiable information of third parties, including real names, addresses, phone numbers, contact details, social media accounts, or personal photos.
3. **Defamation, Harassment, and Discrimination**
   - Persistent defamation, threats, libel, insults, or targeted harassment against specific individuals or groups.
   - Hate speech or discriminatory expressions based on race, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability, beliefs, or other protected characteristics.
4. **Inappropriate Media and Sexual Content**
   - Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and any content that sexualizes minors (**subject to immediate permanent ban and reporting to relevant authorities**).
   - NSFW, grotesque, excessively violent, or sexually explicit images, videos, or external URLs.
5. **Trolling and System Disruption**
   - Flooding, multi-posting, or spamming meaningless strings (intentional thread disruption).
   - Using scripts, bots, or other automated tools to post or place an excessive load on the system.
   - Attempting to circumvent access restrictions (ban evasion), such as using alternate accounts.
6. **Other Prohibited Actions**
   - Impersonating the Operator, Guild Administrators, or other users.
   - Submitting malicious false reports to harass others or obstruct moderation.

## 3. Reporting and Enforcement

- If you encounter a violating post, do not engage with or spread it. Instead, report it using the message context menu or the "Report" button at the bottom of the thread.
- When a violation is confirmed, Guild Administrators or the gobot Operator may take the following actions without prior notice:
  - **Removal / Masking ("abone")**: Hiding or deleting the violating post.
  - **Local Ban**: Restricting access to a specific Guild-Local Board.
  - **Global Ban**: Permanent access ban across all servers for severe trolling, repeated violations, or critical offenses (such as CSAM, criminal threats, or doxxing).
  - **Reporting to Authorities**: In cases involving criminal activity or imminent danger to life or safety, the Operator will report the incident and provide relevant audit information to law enforcement or appropriate authorities.
